About Meridian

Built by clinicians who were tired of hold music too

Meridian Health started in a single clinic in 2011 with one rule: a patient should never have to call three times to book one appointment. Fifteen years on, that rule still runs the place — now across 8 departments, 5 hospitals and 400 doctors.

Every doctor on our platform sets their own real availability. What you see is what you get, down to the minute — and down to your place in the queue.
